Think-Create-Solve!

CSEC ASTU is a Computer Science Engineering Club at Adama Science and Technology University.The club provides mentorship, training, and resources to help students improve their programming skills and participate in programming contests,Bootcamps and Hackathons. By joining the club, students can enhance their problem-solving and critical thinking abilities, improve their coding efficiency, and gain valuable experience.

Mission and Vision

Vision

To inspire and prepare future leaders in science and technology.

Mission

To empower students through knowledge and skills in science, engineering, and computing..

Integrity

Upholding the highest standards of honesty and ethical practices in all we do.

Our Divisions

Competitive Programming Division

The Competitive Programming division is dedicated at enhancing coding skills and fostering a passion for problem-solving among its members. We provide a platform for students to engage in algorithmic challenges and participate in coding competitions at local, national, and international levels.

Cyber Security

The Cyber Security division is dedicated to fostering a community of individuals interested in the field of cybersecurity. Our mission is to educate, engage, and empower members with the knowledge and skills necessary to protect information systems from cyber threats.

Development

We are the web developers. Our mission is to foster a vibrant community of aspiring web developers, where members can learn, collaborate, and grow their skills in web technologies. Whether you're a complete beginner or an experienced coder, you'll find a supportive environment to explore topics like HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and more.

Capacity Building

The Capacity Building Division focuses on empowering individuals and enhancing their skills, knowledge, and abilities. The division aims to provide a supportive environment for students to help them achieve a good result at their academic performance by providing resources and tutorials.
